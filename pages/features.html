Features

Every request in one place.
Clear priorities. Better decisions.

FloTize is work management and productivity software for small and medium businesses, built around the work that arrives in your inbox — not a generic CRM or project tool adapted for it. Here's what that means in practice.

Capture Everything

Nothing arrives untracked

Work arrives by email, phone, client portals, and verbal requests — but there's no single view of what's in progress. FloTize fixes that. Shared inbox emails are automatically turned into trackable requests. Staff log work directly. Recurring jobs are auto-generated on the schedule you set.

  • AI reads and classifies incoming email automatically
  • Extracts client name, phone, and contact details automatically
  • Suggests a category and job type with a confidence score — anything uncertain is flagged for a one-click check
  • Learns from your confirmations and corrections, sender by sender — see, delete, or switch off what it has learned
  • Auto-assigns new requests by category, job type, and current workload
  • Filters out newsletters, auto-replies, and spam
  • Client replies thread back to the original request
  • Recurring work auto-generated — monthly bookkeeping, compliance checks, scheduled reviews
  • Bulk import and export requests by CSV

Full Visibility

See everything. Miss nothing.

Managers can't see true capacity or bottlenecks across the team when work lives in shared inboxes and spreadsheets. FloTize gives you a single view of every request — who owns it, where it stands, and whether it's at risk.

  • Table view for filtering, sorting, and bulk management
  • Kanban board for a visual pipeline overview
  • SLA badges flag overdue and at-risk requests before they breach
  • Team capacity view — see who's overloaded and who has availability
  • Filter by client tier, category, assignee, or date range
  • Overview alerts and a traffic-light health indicator — click any alert to land on exactly those requests
  • Role-based access — Staff, Manager, and Admin — tied to each person's Microsoft login
  • Case number generation and full audit trail on every request

Service Levels

Deadlines that watch themselves

A response target only helps if somebody notices when it slips. FloTize runs every clock automatically and tells you — on the Overview, in the request list, and in your reports — while there is still time to act.

  • First-response and client-reply turnaround targets on every request
  • Time limits per status, plus an overall cycle-time limit
  • Target dates with early warning — and a red flag the moment one passes
  • Quote follow-up and "going cold" reminders
  • Tighter limits for priority clients with per-tier overrides
  • Business days and working hours supported — weekends and public holidays don't count against you, and a "day" can mean a working day
  • SLA reports for response times, cycle time, status limits, and target-date hit rate

Reporting & Improvement

Built-in reporting to drive improvement

Quoting and pricing based on gut feel leads to undercharging for complex work and overcharging for simple work. FloTize gives you the data to get it right — and to improve over time.

  • Effort and cost by work type — see how long different jobs actually take
  • Workload and capacity by person — see who is stretched and who has room, so work is shared fairly
  • Client profitability — know precisely what it costs to service each client
  • Benchmark by category and job type against your top-performing work
  • Pipeline bottlenecks — see where work stalls
  • Revenue by source and category, plus a cost-and-margin trend that shows margin slipping early
  • Parser accuracy — how often the AI's category was kept versus corrected, improving over time
  • AI Insights — highlights what's working and what needs attention
  • Data-grounded quotes — FloTize suggests pricing based on median time for similar jobs

Client Relationships

Every client relationship, in one record

Any team member can pick up a conversation with complete context — full history of every request, every response, every contact. Reach your entire client base, or a targeted subset, directly from the same records.

  • Dedicated client records with contacts, tier, contract details, and tags
  • Full linked history of every request per client
  • Payment terms and client-specific SLA overrides
  • Contract values with start and end dates — contract-covered work isn't double-counted as billable revenue
  • Documents panel — link SharePoint and OneDrive files to each client
  • Bulk email campaigns sent directly from client records, with unsubscribe links built in
  • Filter recipients by tier, status, or tag
  • Personalise with client name and company automatically

Productivity

Less admin. More time for the work that matters.

Small teams lose significant time every week to manual intake, chasing follow-ups and maintaining trackers. FloTize removes that friction — so your people focus on the work that moves the business forward.

📥

Automated intake

Every email is read, classified, and turned into a tracked request automatically. No manual data entry and no copy-pasting between your inbox and a spreadsheet.

🔄

Recurring work, automated

Monthly reports, quarterly checks, annual reviews — auto-generated on the schedule you set. Work appears in your team's queue without anyone having to remember to create it.

📊

See where time actually goes

Log time against specific requests and clients. See utilisation across your team. Identify which work types take longer than expected — and adjust your pricing or process accordingly.

🎯

Right person, automatically

New requests go to someone eligible for that category and job type, balanced by current workload. A hand-picked assignee is never overridden, and you're told if nobody is eligible.

🧠

AI that learns your categories

When the AI isn't sure, it asks. Your confirmations and corrections teach it for that sender — and it only ever advises: what the email actually says always wins.

📅

Calendar and capacity

Schedule work and team activities across several people, log non-workflow time, add public holidays, and see utilisation against the hours people actually have.
