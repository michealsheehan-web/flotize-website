Frequently asked questions

Is there a free trial?

Yes — a free 14-day trial with full access to every feature. No credit card required. We help set up your account so you're working with real requests from day one. If you don't continue, your account becomes read-only for 30 days so you can review and export your data.

How does per-user pricing work?

Starter is $35 per user per month, so your cost follows the size of your team. A user is anyone you add to your account. For example, a team of five pays $175 per month and shares 250 AI drafts and 2,500 emails each month.

What happens if we reach our monthly allowance?

Allowances are pooled across your team and renew every month. We alert your admins at 80% and again at 100%. Emails that arrive while you are at your limit are held rather than lost, and are released automatically once more capacity is available. If you regularly need more, talk to us about a custom plan.

What if we need more than Starter offers?

Larger teams, higher volumes and bespoke needs are handled with a custom plan. Tell us what you need — more AI and email capacity, guided onboarding, a security review, or something particular to your business — and we'll shape a plan with you.

Is there a setup fee?

No. We help you get configured at no cost. If you would like more hands-on support with workflows or training your team, ask us about a custom plan.

What email providers do you support?

FloTize currently works with Microsoft 365 and Outlook through the Microsoft Graph API, and people sign in with their Microsoft work accounts. Support for additional email providers is on our roadmap.

Is my data secure?

FloTize is hosted on Microsoft Azure in the EU, with the database in North Europe (Ireland). Data is encrypted at rest and in transit. We build in tools to support your GDPR obligations — retention policies, right-to-erasure handling and an audit trail. Full details are in our Privacy Policy.

Can I cancel at any time?

Yes — there are no long-term contracts. Cancel anytime and you keep access until the end of your billing period. Contact info@flotize.com to cancel. We delete your data within 30 days of cancellation (copies in backups are removed within a further 7 days), and you can export it as CSV at any time.
