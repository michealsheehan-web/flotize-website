Common questions

What does the 30-minute demo cover?

We walk through your current intake process and show you exactly how FloTize would map to it — email ingestion and AI categorisation, assignment, SLA tracking, reporting, and client management — tailored to your firm type. No generic slide deck.

Do I need Microsoft 365?

For email ingestion and AI drafts, yes — FloTize integrates with Microsoft 365 and Outlook via Microsoft Graph API. Most professional service firms already use M365. If you don't, you can still use FloTize for workflow, client management, and analytics.

How long does setup take?

One day. We configure your account, connect your inbox, and walk your team through the platform. You'll be capturing real work the same day.

What if the AI files a request in the wrong category?

Requests the AI isn't confident about are flagged for a one-click check. When you confirm or change a category, FloTize remembers it for that sender as a hint — it never overrides what an email actually says. You can see, delete, or switch off everything it has learned in Settings.

Is there a free trial?

Yes — 14 days with full access to all features. No credit card required. We set up your account personally so you're capturing real work from day one, not testing an empty system.

What happens to my data if I don't continue?

If you decide FloTize isn't right for you, we export all your data in CSV format and delete your account data within 30 days of cancellation. Any remaining copies in encrypted backups are overwritten within 7 days after that. No lock-in, no data held hostage.
