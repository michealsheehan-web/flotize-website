Common questions

What does the 30-minute demo cover?

We walk through your current intake process and show you how FloTize would fit it — email ingestion and AI categorisation, assignment, deadline tracking, reporting and client management — tailored to your type of business. No generic slide deck.

Do I need Microsoft 365?

Yes, today. FloTize captures email through Microsoft 365 and Outlook (Microsoft Graph API), and people sign in with their Microsoft work accounts. Support for other email providers is on our roadmap.

How long does setup take?

Typically about a day. We help configure your account, connect your inbox and walk your team through the platform, so you can start capturing real work quickly.

What if the AI files a request in the wrong category?

Requests the AI isn't confident about are flagged for a one-click check. When you confirm or change a category, FloTize remembers it for that sender as a hint — it never overrides what an email actually says. You can see, delete, or switch off everything it has learned in Settings.

Is there a free trial?

Yes — 14 days with full access to every feature. No credit card required. We help set up your account so you're working with real requests from day one, not testing an empty system.

What happens to my data if I don't continue?

After a trial ends, your account stays read-only for 30 days so you can review and export your data as CSV. If you don't continue, we delete your data within 30 days (copies in backups are removed within a further 7 days). No lock-in, no data held hostage.
