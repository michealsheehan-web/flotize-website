About FloTize

Built for businesses whose
work arrives by email

FloTize started with a simple observation: small and medium businesses run their work from shared inboxes, spreadsheets and sticky notes — with no single view of what is in progress, who is overloaded, or where time and money are going.

Our story

We built the tool we wished existed

Accountancy practices, law firms, consultancies, agencies, property businesses and trades all face the same core problem: work arrives from multiple directions, gets managed across multiple tools, and nobody has a clear view of capacity, delivery or profitability.

Larger organisations have had sophisticated work-management tools for years. We built FloTize to bring that same capability — with modern AI — to the small and medium businesses that power local economies. Not a generic CRM or project tool adapted for the job, but a productivity tool that starts where the work actually starts: in your inbox.

FloTize is built on Microsoft Azure, uses Azure OpenAI for its AI features, and works with Microsoft 365. It's designed to be set up quickly, not over months — and to give you clearer priorities, better visibility and less operational friction from the start.

Our mission

Capture → Prioritize → Improve

That's the FloTize philosophy. Capture every piece of work automatically and consistently. Prioritize it intelligently — with deadlines, capacity visibility and client tier awareness. Then improve continuously — with data-grounded insights into your clients, work types and staff workload that make the business run better and your clients better served.
