About FloTize

Built for firms that run on
client relationships and email

FloTize started with a simple observation: professional service firms were managing complex client work from shared inboxes, spreadsheets, and sticky notes — with no single view of what was in progress, who was overloaded, or which clients were profitable.

Our story

We built the tool we wished existed

Accountancy firms, law practices, financial advisors, and consultancies all face the same core problem: work arrives from multiple directions, gets managed across multiple tools, and nobody has a clear view of capacity, delivery, or profitability.

The large firms have had sophisticated workflow tools for years. We built FloTize to bring that same capability — with modern AI — to the small and medium professional service firms that power local economies. Not a generic CRM adapted for service work. A platform built from the ground up for how these firms actually operate.

FloTize is built on Microsoft Azure, uses GPT-4o for AI features, and integrates natively with Microsoft 365. It's designed to be set up in a day, not a month — and to cut admin, speed up responses, and give you the data to improve your margins.
