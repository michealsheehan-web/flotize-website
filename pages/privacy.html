Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Who we are

FloTize LLC ("FloTize", "we", "our", "us"), a Texas limited liability company, provides work management and productivity software for small and medium businesses. Our postal address is PO Box 90611, Austin, Texas 78709, United States. For the client data you put into FloTize, you are the controller and we are your processor. Our contact email is info@flotize.com.

2. What data we collect

We collect and process the following categories of data:

  • Account data: Name, email address, and business details provided when signing up or contacting us
  • Usage data: Information about how you use the FloTize platform, including feature usage and login activity
  • Client data you upload: Information about your clients that you input or that is imported via email ingestion — including names, email addresses, phone numbers, and correspondence
  • Email content: Emails processed by the FloTize AI ingestion feature are read and analysed to create workflow requests
  • Email categorisation memory: If the email-parser learning feature is on, we store, per sender, a tally of the categories your team assigned, plus a short sample (subject and the start of the email) from a limited number of recently corrected emails. This is used only to improve categorisation for your own account. You can view, delete, or switch it off at any time in Settings, and it is covered by the retention and deletion terms in section 7.
  • Technical data: IP addresses, browser type, and device information collected automatically

3. How we use your data

  • To provide and operate the FloTize platform
  • To process client enquiries via AI email ingestion
  • To generate AI-powered responses and insights
  • To send service-related communications
  • To comply with legal obligations

4. Data storage and security

Your data is stored and processed on Microsoft Azure infrastructure in the European Union: the database is hosted in North Europe (Ireland), the application and file storage in West Europe (Netherlands), and AI processing uses Azure OpenAI hosted in Sweden Central. Data is encrypted at rest and in transit. We use industry-standard security practices and access controls to protect your data. Because FloTize is based in the United States, our staff may access data stored in the EU from there. For customers in the EU and UK, we rely on the EU Standard Contractual Clauses and the UK International Data Transfer Addendum for this access.

5. AI processing

FloTize uses Azure OpenAI to classify incoming emails, extract information, and generate suggested responses. Email content is processed by Azure OpenAI in accordance with Microsoft's data processing terms. We do not use your data to train AI models. See How FloTize uses AI for details, including how Microsoft handles AI requests.

6. Your rights under GDPR

If you are based in the EU or UK, you have the following rights:

  • Right to access your personal data
  • Right to rectify inaccurate data
  • Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten")
  • Right to restrict processing
  • Right to data portability
  • Right to object to processing

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at info@flotize.com.

7. Data retention

We retain your account and usage data for as long as your account is active. Client data within the FloTize platform is retained according to the data retention policies you configure within the platform. Upon account cancellation, your data is deleted within 30 days unless you ask us to delete it sooner, and any remaining copies held in encrypted backups are removed within a further 7 days. After a free trial ends, your account stays read-only for 30 days so you can review and export your data; if you do not continue, the same deletion timescales apply. You can export your data as CSV at any time.

8. Third-party services

FloTize integrates with the following third-party services. The full list of sub-processors, with locations, is on our sub-processors page.

  • Microsoft Azure — cloud infrastructure and storage
  • Microsoft Graph API — email integration
  • Azure OpenAI — AI processing

9. Cookies

The FloTize website uses only essential cookies required for the site to function. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

10. Business contacts and outreach

We may contact people at businesses that could benefit from FloTize. We use business details such as name, work email address, job title and company, taken from public sources, LinkedIn and business contact databases such as Apollo. We do this because we have a legitimate interest in telling businesses about our software. Every message says who we are and how to opt out, and we stop contacting anyone who asks. We keep a short record of opt-outs so we do not contact you again. If we obtained your details from a third party, our first message tells you where from. To ask what we hold about you, or to have it deleted, contact info@flotize.com.

11. Contact

For any privacy-related questions or requests, contact us at info@flotize.com or by post at FloTize LLC, PO Box 90611, Austin, Texas 78709, United States.
