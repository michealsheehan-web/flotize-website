How it works

One place to capture, prioritize and improve all your work

FloTize connects your inbox, your team and your clients into a single workflow — so nothing gets missed, everyone knows what to do next, and you can see how to run the business better.

1

Capture everything automatically

Emails become tracked requests as they arrive. Staff can log work directly. Recurring jobs — monthly reports, scheduled reviews, regular check-ups — are generated on the schedule you set. Nothing arrives untracked. When the AI isn't sure of a category, it flags the request for a one-click check — and learns from your answer.

2

Prioritize with full visibility

See what your team can realistically handle, where capacity is spare or stretched, and which deadlines are approaching. New requests go to the right person automatically, and Overview alerts show what's slipping before it does. View work as a table or a Kanban board, filtered by client, category or assignee.

3

Respond faster with AI

FloTize drafts replies based on your own knowledge base and past responses, so answers reflect how your business actually operates — not a generic model. Edit or send as-is — your choice.

4

Improve through insight

Track effort and cost by work type, client and staff member. See workload and capacity across the team, benchmark job types, and get AI-generated insights on what's working and where time and money are being lost.
