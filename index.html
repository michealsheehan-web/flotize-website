How it works

One place to capture, track, and deliver all your work

FloTize connects your email, your team, and your clients into a single intelligent workflow — so nothing gets missed and every client gets the service level they expect.

1

Capture everything automatically

Emails are turned into tracked requests within minutes. Staff can log work directly. Recurring jobs — monthly bookkeeping, compliance checks, scheduled reviews — are auto-generated on the schedule you set. Nothing arrives untracked. When the AI isn't sure of a category, it flags the request for a one-click check — and learns from your answer.

2

Prioritize with full visibility

See what your team can realistically handle, where capacity is unused or overstretched, and which SLA deadlines are approaching. New requests go to the right person automatically, and Overview alerts show what's slipping before it does. View work as a detailed table or a Kanban board — filtered by client, category, or assignee.

3

Respond faster with AI

FloTize drafts replies based on your own knowledge base, past responses, and engagement letters. Answers reflect how your firm actually operates — not a generic model. Edit or send as-is — your choice.

4

Improve through better data

Track effort and cost by work type, client, and staff member. Benchmark performance across job types. Get AI-generated insights that surface what's working and where margins are being lost.
